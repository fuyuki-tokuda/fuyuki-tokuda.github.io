About

Fuyuki Tokuda

I am an Assistant Professor at the Unprecedented-scale Data Analytics Center, Tohoku University. My work in robot vision and manipulation spans precise image-guided positioning, deformable object handling and automated sewing systems.

After completing my Ph.D. in Engineering at Tohoku University, I worked on garment automation at TransGP and The University of Hong Kong. I am interested in bringing perception and control methods together in complete, working robotic systems.
