Sewing without dedicated fixtures

Dual arms regulate fabric position and tension while vision tracks the seam line, joining two stacked fabric pieces without custom fixtures.

The challenge

Fixture-based sewing requires adjustments or redesigned tooling when fabric geometry changes.

The approach

The robot holds the fabric from both sides and coordinates fabric motion with each stitch. Visual feedback tracks the printed seam line, while internal force keeps the fabric flat.

Evaluation and scope

Real-robot experiments evaluated straight and curved seams on aligned, stacked fabric pieces. The later ICMA 2025 work extends automation to the surrounding handling stages.
