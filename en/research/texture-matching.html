Aligning fabric textures

Transformer-based visual servoing aligns fabric patterns. Coordinated force control keeps the fabric under tension during alignment and placement.

The challenge

Aligning two fabric pieces requires matching their surface patterns while limiting deformation during handling.

The approach

A Difference Extraction Attention Module (DEAM) estimates relative pose from image pairs. Visual servoing and dual-arm impedance control coordinate alignment and placement.

Evaluation and scope

A model trained on synthetic images was evaluated on real fabrics with different textures, without prior training on the specific test textures.
