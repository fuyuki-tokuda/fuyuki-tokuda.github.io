Learning motion from differences between images

A target image and a current image guide precise positioning with an external camera.

The challenge

Visual feedback must turn differences in appearance into robot motions that reduce pose error.

The approach

DEFINet encodes both images with shared CNN weights and regresses relative pose from the difference between their features. Training pairs are collected by moving the manipulator.

Evaluation and scope

Evaluation used simulation and a real six-degree-of-freedom industrial manipulator. This line of work includes a 2019 SICE journal paper and the 2021 IEEE Access article.
