From fabric picking to completed seams

An integrated system connects picking, stacking, loading, sewing and unloading without dedicated fixtures.

The challenge

Automating stitches alone leaves manual work before and after sewing. Fabric handling must connect reliably with the sewing operation.

The approach

The system combines dual arms, rolling-up end-effectors, a sewing head, force sensors and cameras under a single PC. Segmentation, pose estimation and trajectory calculation support the sequence for relatively thin fabrics.

Evaluation and scope

Experiments demonstrated automated handling and stitching along several predefined seam lines under the reported fabric and task conditions.

First-author publication
