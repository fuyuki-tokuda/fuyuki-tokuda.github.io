Positioning and flattening fabric together

Images and force/torque measurements guide a dual-arm robot to position a fabric piece while removing wrinkles.

The challenge

Moving fabric also changes its shape. Wrinkles on untextured fabric are difficult to observe in a conventional grayscale image.

The approach

Structured lighting highlights surface shape. A multimodal CNN uses images and force/torque data to predict manipulator motion, learning from data collected on the real system.

Evaluation and scope

Real-robot experiments tested simultaneous positioning and flattening with previously unseen wrinkles.
