Research

From image-guided positioning to tasks involving fabric deformation and contact. The projects below focus on my first-author robotics work.

IEEE RA-L / 2026

Aligning fabric textures

Transformer-based visual servoing aligns fabric patterns. Coordinated force control keeps the fabric under tension during alignment and placement.

From fabric picking to completed seams

Robotic Sewing System for Fully Automated Fabric Picking, Stacking, Loading, Sewing, and Unloading

An integrated system connects picking, stacking, loading, sewing and unloading without dedicated fixtures.

IEEE TASE / 2025

Sewing without dedicated fixtures

Fixture-Free 2D Sewing Using a Dual-Arm Manipulator System

Dual arms regulate fabric position and tension while vision tracks the seam line, joining two stacked fabric pieces without custom fixtures.

Positioning and flattening fabric together

CNN-based Visual Servoing for Simultaneous Positioning and Flattening of Soft Fabric Parts

Images and force/torque measurements guide a dual-arm robot to position a fabric piece while removing wrinkles.

Ongoing research

Current directions

Learning with visual feedback

I am investigating generative models and imitation learning for precise deformable object manipulation, including how visual feedback can complement vision-language-action (VLA) models.

Shape estimation and closed-loop control
